# Starter Website Agreement Template

This is a practical starter template, not legal advice. Have a qualified professional review it before relying on it for paid client work.

## Parties

Provider: [Your legal name or business name]
Client: [Client legal name or business name]

## Services

Provider will design and build the website described in the attached scope.

## Fees

Client will pay:
- Deposit: [amount]
- Final payment: [amount]
- Optional maintenance: [amount]/month

Work begins after deposit is received.

## Revisions

The project includes [number] revision rounds. Extra revisions are billed at [rate] or quoted separately.

## Client Content

Client is responsible for providing accurate text, images, logos, business details, legal notices, and permissions for any reviews or testimonials used.

Client is responsible for checking privacy, cookie, consumer-law, regulated-claim, license, and industry-specific requirements for their own business before launch.

## Ownership

After final payment, Client owns the final website files created specifically for the project. Provider may reuse general methods, layouts, code patterns, and know-how.

## Third-Party Services

Client is responsible for costs and accounts for domains, hosting, email, analytics, form providers, booking tools, payment processors, and other third-party services unless agreed in writing.

## Maintenance

Maintenance is not included after launch unless a monthly plan is selected. Maintenance scope must be documented separately.

## No Guaranteed Results

Provider does not guarantee search rankings, sales, revenue, ad results, or specific lead volume.

## Portfolio Use

Provider may display the completed work in a portfolio unless Client requests otherwise in writing.

## Cancellation

If Client cancels after work begins, the deposit is non-refundable and any completed work beyond the deposit may be invoiced.
