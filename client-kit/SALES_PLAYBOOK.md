# Locriva Sales Playbook

## Positioning

Locriva builds simple, fast websites for small businesses that need more calls, quote requests, bookings, or local trust.

Do not pitch complicated software first. Pitch clarity, speed, and fewer lost leads.

## Best First Customers

- Business has an outdated website.
- Business has no clear contact form or booking CTA.
- Business depends on local trust.
- Owner is reachable by email, Instagram, Facebook, or phone.
- Website problem is obvious in under 2 minutes.

## First Offer

Starter Website:
- One-page website.
- Mobile responsive.
- Contact or quote form.
- Basic SEO titles and descriptions.
- Launch checklist.
- Suggested pilot price: EUR 499.

Care Plan:
- Monthly link/form check.
- Small content edits.
- Analytics snapshot.
- Suggested first price: EUR 49-EUR 99/month.

## Outreach Message

Subject: quick website idea for [Business Name]

Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific issue: mobile layout, unclear services, no quote form, old design, slow first impression].

I build simple websites for small businesses that make the offer clearer and make it easier for customers to contact you. I put together a few demo directions here: [Locriva link].

Would you be open to a quick homepage teardown or a simple redesign direction for [Business Name]?

Thanks,
[Your Name]

## Sales Call Structure

1. What do you want the website to do?
2. Where do leads come from today?
3. What services are most profitable?
4. What questions do customers ask before buying?
5. What websites do you like or dislike?
6. What photos, reviews, and proof can you provide?
7. Timeline and budget.
8. Confirm scope and next step.

## When To Walk Away

- Client wants guaranteed SEO rankings.
- Client wants unlimited work for a fixed tiny price.
- Client will not provide content, photos, or feedback.
- Client refuses written scope or payment terms.
- Client wants fake reviews, copied content, or misleading claims.

## Simple Pipeline Statuses

- Researching
- Contacted
- Followed up
- Call booked
- Quote sent
- Won
- Lost
