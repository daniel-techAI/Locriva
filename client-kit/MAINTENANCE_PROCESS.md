# Maintenance Process

## Monthly Routine

1. Confirm the domain, SSL certificate, and main pages load.
2. Open the site on current desktop and mobile browsers.
3. Test the real contact, booking, phone, email, map, payment, and quote paths.
4. Check navigation, footer, legal pages, broken links, missing images, and browser-console errors.
5. Review performance and accessibility regressions on the most important pages.
6. Apply included client edits and record exactly what changed.
7. Review consent, analytics, form storage, and privacy notices if any provider changed.
8. Review analytics only when the client has approved the tool and the legal setup supports it.
9. Back up client-owned content or configuration where the agreement includes backups.
10. Send the monthly summary and one evidence-based improvement recommendation.

## Edit Rules

Included:
- Text changes.
- Replacing images.
- Updating hours.
- Adding a small section.
- Updating CTA links.

Not included:
- Full redesign.
- New website pages beyond plan.
- New app features.
- Emergency support unless sold separately.
- SEO guarantee.
- Third-party subscription fees.
- Recovery from client-caused account loss unless separately agreed.
- Legal review or writing regulated industry claims.
