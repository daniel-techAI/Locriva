# Legal And Business Checklist

This is not legal or tax advice. It is a checklist of topics to resolve before taking real money.

## Business Setup

- Decide whether you are operating as an individual/sole proprietor or registered business.
- Check whether you need local business registration.
- Set up a way to invoice clients.
- Set up a business bank/payment method.
- Track income and expenses from day one.
- Understand tax obligations in your country/state.

## Website Compliance

- Use real contact/business information where required.
- Do not use fake reviews or fake testimonials.
- Get permission before using client logos, reviews, or photos.
- Use a privacy policy when collecting form submissions or analytics data.
- If serving EU/Germany, check GDPR and Impressum requirements.
- Do not guarantee SEO rankings or business results.

## Client Protection

- Use written scope before starting.
- Take a deposit.
- Define revision rounds.
- Define who owns domain and hosting accounts.
- Define maintenance responsibilities.
- Keep a launch checklist.

## Inputs Needed From You

- Country/state where you will operate.
- Your preferred business name.
- Your contact email.
- Payment method you can accept.
- Whether you want to sell in English, German, or both.
