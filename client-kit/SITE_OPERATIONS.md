# Site Operations

Use this checklist whenever Locriva or a client website is changed.

## Before Editing

1. Confirm which site, branch, and public URL are being changed.
2. Record the requested result and what is explicitly outside scope.
3. Keep a copy of client text, images, access details, and approvals in the agreed project location.
4. Confirm that supplied assets may legally be used.

## Before Publishing

1. Review desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts.
2. Test navigation, forms, phone links, email links, booking links, and downloads.
3. Check every internal page, image path, anchor target, and footer link.
4. Check required labels, prices, hours, service area, contact details, privacy, and legal pages.
5. Check keyboard focus, headings, form labels, alt text, color contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
6. Check browser-console errors and page weight; compress new images to modern formats.
7. Confirm analytics, cookies, embeds, payments, and external providers match the approved privacy setup.
8. Publish, then repeat the conversion-path tests on the real public URL.

## Client Accounts

- Keep the domain, hosting, business email, analytics, booking, and payment accounts in the client's name whenever practical.
- Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication.
- Do not place real passwords in ordinary forms, issue trackers, or unprotected documents.
- Keep a written access and ownership list for handover.

## Incident Routine

1. Record what failed, when it started, and who reported it.
2. Confirm whether the issue affects availability, leads, payments, personal data, or only presentation.
3. Preserve evidence before changing systems.
4. Restore the most important customer path first.
5. Tell the client what is known, what is not known, and the next update time.
6. Review whether a provider, privacy notice, security measure, or maintenance scope must change.
