Terms of Service

These terms explain the basic rules for using this website and discussing work with GrowthStack. A paid project should always use a written scope, quote, or agreement before work starts.

Website purpose

GrowthStack presents website design, launch support, and optional maintenance services for small businesses. The demo sites are sample portfolio concepts. They are not real clients, real testimonials, or proof of past client results.

No automatic contract

Submitting a form, sending an email, or using a demo page does not create a paid contract. A project begins only when both sides agree on scope, price, timeline, payment terms, and responsibilities in writing.

Project scope

Every paid project should define the pages, features, content responsibilities, revisions, launch support, hosting/domain responsibilities, third-party tools, and what is excluded. Work outside the agreed scope can be quoted separately.

Client responsibilities

  • Provide accurate business details, service information, images, and legal content.
  • Confirm that supplied images, logos, text, and brand assets may legally be used.
  • Review drafts and give feedback in a reasonable time.
  • Pay agreed invoices on time.
  • Approve final content before launch.

Payments and refunds

Payment terms must be stated in the written quote or agreement. Typical project work may require a deposit before build work starts. Custom design and development work is usually not refundable once delivered or once substantial work has begun, unless the written agreement says otherwise or mandatory law applies.

Maintenance plans

Maintenance plans are recurring support services with limited monthly scope. They do not include unlimited redesigns, paid advertising, emergency 24/7 support, guaranteed SEO rankings, or work that requires a separate project quote.

No guarantees

GrowthStack can improve clarity, speed, structure, contact paths, and basic search setup, but it does not guarantee search rankings, sales, revenue, ad performance, exact lead volume, or platform uptime controlled by third parties.

Portfolio use

Finished client work may be used in GrowthStack's portfolio only with permission or where allowed by the written agreement. Private business information should not be published without approval.

Consumer contracts

GrowthStack is currently positioned for business-to-business projects. If services are sold to consumers, extra consumer-law information, cancellation rights, and withdrawal wording may be required before accepting the order.
