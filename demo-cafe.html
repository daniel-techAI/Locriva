Our story

Built for the neighborhood. Made with care.

North Table began as a Saturday market stall and grew into a permanent home for generous breakfasts, thoughtful coffee, and unhurried lunches.

We buy from producers we know, change the menu when the seasons do, and keep enough familiar favorites that a weekday coffee still feels easy.
