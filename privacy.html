Privacy Policy

This policy explains how GrowthStack handles personal data submitted through this website, email, and project communication. It is written for the current public preview setup: a static website hosted on GitHub Pages with email-based enquiries and no payment checkout.

Who controls the data

GrowthStack is the website project name currently operated by Daniel through daniellaky.uni@gmail.com. Formal business registration, VAT, and invoicing details must be added here before paid client contracting if required by the country where the business is registered.

What data is collected

  • Email address and any contact details you choose to send.
  • Business name, business type, website/social link, budget range, timeline, and project goals.
  • Feedback submitted through the preview hub.
  • Email messages, attachments, meeting notes, and project files shared during client work.
  • Basic technical hosting logs may be processed by GitHub Pages or future hosting providers.

Why the data is used

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare quotes.
  • To understand project requirements and decide whether the work is a good fit.
  • To deliver agreed website design, support, and maintenance services.
  • To keep simple project records, invoices, and legal/accounting records where required.
  • To improve the website and service based on voluntary feedback.

Legal basis

Enquiry data is processed to take steps before entering a contract or because GrowthStack has a legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. Client project data is processed to perform a contract. Some records may be kept to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations.

Cookies and analytics

This site currently does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, embedded social trackers, Google Analytics, or externally hosted web fonts. If analytics or marketing tools are added later, the cookie policy and consent flow must be updated before those tools go live.

Third parties

This website is currently published through GitHub Pages. Form buttons on GitHub Pages open an email draft to daniellaky.uni@gmail.com instead of storing submissions in a website database. Future hosting, form, analytics, or payment providers must be reviewed before use.

How long data is kept

Unsuccessful enquiries are normally deleted when they are no longer useful, unless a longer period is needed for dispute prevention or legal reasons. Client records may be kept for the period required by accounting, tax, and contract rules.

Your rights

Depending on the situation, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to processing of your personal data. You can request this by emailing daniellaky.uni@gmail.com.

Important note

This policy is a practical starter policy, not legal advice. It should be reviewed by a qualified professional before GrowthStack starts paid client work at scale, uses paid ads, stores form submissions, uses tracking analytics, or sells to consumers.
