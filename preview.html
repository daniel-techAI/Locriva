Feedback

Ask people for useful criticism.

Do not ask, "Do you like it?" Ask questions that reveal whether this can sell, where trust breaks, and what needs to be clearer.

Score the trust

Which page feels most believable, and which part still feels fake, generic, or unclear?

Repeat the offer

Can the reviewer explain what Locriva sells after 30 seconds on the homepage?

Compare the demos

Ask which demo feels most real and which one still feels closest to a template.

Find the action

Can they find how to request a project, request a quote, book, reserve, or contact?
