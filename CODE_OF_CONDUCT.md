# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Locriva contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation welcoming, respectful, and constructive. Be considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels, give actionable feedback, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, spam, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `daniellaky.uni@gmail.com` with the subject **Locriva conduct report**. Include relevant links or screenshots and enough context to understand the concern. Reports will be handled as privately as practical and reviewed on a best-effort basis.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, issue a warning, temporarily limit participation, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces when necessary. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is not acceptable.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
